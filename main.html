Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Momentum and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the billing period.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the app and look for Restore Purchases on the subscription screen or in your profile settings. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. If it still doesn't work, email us.

How does the AI Coach work?

The AI Coach gives training suggestions based on the workouts you log. It's meant for general fitness guidance and is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have an injury or health condition.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us from the address you used in the app with the subject Delete my data. We'll confirm and remove your data.
